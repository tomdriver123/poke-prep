Poke Prep

Poke Prep turns interview prep into a Gen 1 Pokemon run. Every town on the Kanto map is a topic, every encounter is a quiz question from that topic's question bank, and a correct answer is a catch (or XP, if the Pokemon is already in your pokedex). A wrong answer and the Pokemon flees. Gym towns level the party for their track, and the trainer card shows the party, the pokedex and the XP behind each level.

Your progress lives in plain Markdown files (a pokedex ledger and one small file per answer), so the game is a view over notes you own. Point the server at your own vault to play with your own questions, or start with the sample vault that ships with the repo.

A fan project. Not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokemon Company. Species data comes from PokeAPI and sprites are loaded from the PokeAPI sprites repository.
